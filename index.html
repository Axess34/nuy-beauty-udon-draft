About us · เกี่ยวกับเรา

Healthy hair, colour you'll love.

Nuy Beauty is a friendly family-run studio in the heart of Udon Thani. P'Nuy (พี่หนุ่ย) has been cutting and colouring for more than 20 years and trained at the Ketwadee Gandini academy (สถาบันเกตุวดีแกนดินี่).

We check the condition of your hair before every service and recommend the colour and care that suit you, from soft earth tones and ash browns to bright blondes. Many of our clients have been coming back for years.

  • Over 20 years' hair experience
  • Japanese professional colour lines
  • Hair, brows, lips & lashes in one place
